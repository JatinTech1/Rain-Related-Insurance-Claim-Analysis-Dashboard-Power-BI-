# Rain-Related-Insurance-Claim-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI-
Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of rain-related insurance claims.
It helps insurance companies and analysts understand the financial impact of rainfall on various claim types, identify fraud trends, and explore insights across multiple dimensions such as claim type, policy type, and state.

## Objectives

To analyze the total claim amount across different rain categories.

To understand the distribution of claims by type and status.

To identify fraud vs. non-fraud patterns.

To assess average claim amounts by claim type and region.

To enable interactive exploration using slicers for deeper insights.

## Key Visualizations

Total Claim Amount by Rain Category – Displays the monetary impact of various rain severity levels.

Distribution of Claims by Type – Shows how different claim types (Health, Vehicle, Property, etc.) contribute to overall claims.

Distribution of Claims by Status – Visualizes proportions of approved, pending, and rejected claims.

Total Claim Amount by State – Identifies high-claim regions affected by rain.

Count of Claims by Type and Status – Highlights which claim types face more approvals or rejections.

Fraud vs. Non-Fraud Claims (%) – Shows the proportion of fraudulent claims in the dataset.

Average Claim Amount by Claim Type – Helps identify which claim categories have higher claim values.

## Filters & Slicers

Rain Category (e.g., Light, Moderate, Heavy)

Claim Status (Approved, Rejected, Pending)

Policy Type

State

These slicers make the dashboard interactive and allow users to explore claims from different perspectives.

## Insights

Heavy rain events tend to drive higher claim amounts across all categories.

Vehicle and property damage claims are most common during heavy rainfall.

Certain states show higher fraud claim percentages, requiring stricter verification.

The dashboard helps identify seasonal trends and risk-prone areas for insurance companies.

## Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI Desktop – Data visualization and dashboard design

Microsoft Excel / CSV – Data source

DAX – Calculations and measures

Data Cleaning & Transformation – Power Query

## Conclusion

This dashboard provides actionable insights into rain-related insurance claims, helping insurers:

Detect potential fraud

Optimize policy strategies

Improve claim processing efficiency

Understand weather-related risk exposure
